Frequently Asked Questions

How do I run MusicGan after downloading?
Extract MusicGan-v1.2.zip to any folder of your choice and launch MusicGan.exe. You can also run create-desktop-shortcut.bat once to place an icon directly on your Windows desktop.
Does MusicGan require administrator privileges?
No. MusicGan runs entirely in standard user space without touching system directories, registry hives, or requiring UAC prompts.
Is macOS supported?
This build is engineered natively for Windows 10 and 11 using WPF. A companion macOS edition built with native Swift and SwiftUI designed to dock against the MacBook hardware notch is currently in development.
